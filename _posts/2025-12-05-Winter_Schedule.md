Cornell's fall semester is quickly coming to an end, and Teagle Hall is closing quite early this year.
While we do not have to shift our class times at all, our last class will be on Sunday December 14th.

We encourage all our students who will be remaining in Ithaca to attend classes at Finger Lakes Aikido throughout the break, Mondays and Fridays at 6pm.
I hope to see you there!

We will post the January schedule once we know Teagle Hall's hours, but we expect our classes will start at the same time as regular classes on campus.
